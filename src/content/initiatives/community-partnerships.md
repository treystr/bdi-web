Building a more prosperous and financially sovereign DC starts with empowering the organizations that serve our community. Through our Strategic Partnerships initiative, we collaborate with local nonprofits and community groups to integrate Bitcoin into their long-term financial strategy.

By providing the education and tools needed to adopt Bitcoin as a strategic asset, we help these organizations build lasting resilience, ensuring they have the resources to continue their vital work for years to come.

Together, we are laying the groundwork for a more stable and independent future for all of Washington, DC.
